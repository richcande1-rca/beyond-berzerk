SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

Hold SPACE to fire. Hold ← / ↑ to rotate counterclockwise, or → / ↓ to rotate clockwise through 360°, with smooth acceleration and braking. Q / E work too. Mouse/touchpad points to a target direction; the turret rotates toward it at the same maximum speed as the keyboard (no instant turns). WASD selects one of eight target directions using that same speed limit.

On touchscreens, drag on the arena to steer and fire, or use the quick-aim pad with the FIRE button. Touch aiming and quick-aim buttons also turn the turret at the shared maximum speed.

TEST MODE: Enter any wave from 1 to 999 beside the PAUSE button, then press LAUNCH TEST. This begins a new run at that wave with the normal enemy mix and 3 lives. Test scores never replace your personal best.

Each life withstands three projectile hits; robot contact still costs an entire life. Normal projectiles stop at walls, but the later Penetrator can shoot through them. Your lasers can shoot down enemy projectiles. Robots flank, rush, and scatter during chain explosions. Graduation upgrades: Wave 3 rechargeable shield; Wave 4 twin cannons (available from Wave 5) and Heavy Laser if you score eight direct robot kills during Wave 4; Wave 6 periodically charged Penetrator. During Wave 4, one glowing shield capsule flies through the walls; shoot it through an open lane to refill all three shield charges. The direct-kill counter appears in Wave 4's HUD. Every conquered wave gets a diploma and fanfare before unveiling new wall architecture: the solid wall segments, shooting lanes, and matching collision geometry now differ by sector. SPIN → MORPH → MELTDOWN remain playable.
