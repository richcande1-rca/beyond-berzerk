SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

Hold SPACE to fire. Hold ← / ↑ to rotate counterclockwise, or → / ↓ to rotate clockwise through 360°, with smooth acceleration and braking. Q / E work too. Mouse/touchpad points to a target direction; the turret rotates toward it at the same maximum speed as the keyboard (no instant turns). WASD selects one of eight target directions using that same speed limit.

On phones, drag the dedicated left aim wheel with one thumb and hold the right FIRE button with the other. Both work together without covering the action; dragging on the arena still aims and fires. On desktop the original quick-aim pad and controls remain unchanged. All aiming uses the same maximum turn speed.

TEST MODE: Enter any wave from 1 to 999 beside the PAUSE button, then press LAUNCH TEST. This begins a new run at that wave with the normal enemy mix and 3 lives. Test scores never replace your personal best.

Each life withstands three projectile hits; robot contact still costs an entire life. Normal projectiles stop at walls, but the Wave 7 automatic Railgun launches a massive gold blast straight through them every 4.5 seconds while ordinary bullets continue firing. Your lasers can shoot down enemy projectiles. Robots flank, rush, and scatter during chain explosions. Graduation upgrades: Wave 3 rechargeable shield; Wave 4 twin cannons (available from Wave 5) and Heavy Laser if you score eight direct robot kills during Wave 4; Wave 6 Railgun (automatic from Wave 7). Supply drones cross during combat: shoot a gold ? for temporary Triple Threat, Guardian Drones, or Stasis; shoot a green + for hull repair (or +250 points if full), or a rare blue bolt to recharge shields and any unlocked Railgun. Extra lives are awarded every 15,000 score, up to five held. The six-hit Sentinel interrupts Wave 3 halfway through its swarm; the ten-hit Breaker brings twin-cannon volleys and charging assaults in Wave 7. Stronger mini-bosses return every four waves after that, blocking graduation until defeated and dropping a bonus supply. Bruisers from Wave 5 onward have 5–7 armored HP and erupt into three-shot rage attacks when damaged. Wave 12 introduces THE LEVIATHAN, a major boss with its own five-stage transparent pixel-art sprite sheet, three individually targetable four-hit weapon pods, capped robot reinforcements and a final enraged charge. Destroy every pod and then land six hits on its exposed reactor core; defeating it awards 4,000 points before Wave 13. Timed powers expire automatically; their timer appears below the arena. During Wave 4, a shield capsule makes one wall-phasing pass after 90% of the robots are eliminated; shoot it through an open lane to refill all three shield charges. Once the swarm and capsule pass are finished, THE WARDEN enters: three hits on its periodically exposed cyan reactor defeat it. Both bosses now expose their cores for shorter windows in later phases and become increasingly unpredictable as they take damage. Only then does Wave 4 graduate and award twin cannons. Wave 8's ARCHITECT boss can reverse and accelerate the moving walls; shoot its exposed violet core four times, using the Railgun to pierce blocked lanes. Defeating it unlocks an automatic chain-lightning Arc Cannon for Wave 9. The direct-kill counter appears in Wave 4's HUD. Each sector now has a distinct visual identity: crystalline Prism, emerald Vector circuits, ruined Phantom machinery, folding Kaleidoscope mirrors, and deep-space Cosmos. Every conquered wave gets a diploma and fanfare before unveiling new wall architecture: the solid wall segments, shooting lanes, and matching collision geometry now differ by sector. SPIN → MORPH → MELTDOWN remain playable.
